# Hakbang PH model card

Last updated: 28 July 2026
Model/data version: `PH-SKILLS-FIRST-SYN-2200-2026.07.28`

## Intended use

Hakbang PH is a learning and career-exploration prototype. It returns up to three
comparative job-family options from a fixed catalogue of 11 pathways, along with
skills to build, AI-era work guidance, research sources, and official learning
links.

It is not suitable for hiring, screening, promotion, redundancy, compensation,
immigration, education admission, or other high-impact decisions. It does not
estimate employment probability, salary, promotion readiness, or career success.

## Data and features

The training set contains 2,200 deterministic synthetic rows: 200 rows for each
of 11 job-family labels. Rows are generated from documented prototypes with
seeded noise and deliberate adjacent-family overlap.

Model inputs:

- total years of work experience; and
- 12 skill ratings on a 0–5 scale, where 0 means no experience.

Excluded inputs include industry, current job title, employer, career goal,
leadership years, age, sex, gender, race, religion, disability, marital status,
and other protected attributes.

The dataset contains no real résumés, people, employers, vacancies, or career
outcomes. Its label is produced by the same prototype system that generates its
features, so cross-validation mainly measures recovery of simulation rules.

## Recommendation pipeline

1. Candidate classifiers are compared with seeded stratified five-fold
   cross-validation. Extra Trees currently has the strongest synthetic macro-F1.
2. The fitted classifier produces a relative synthetic-model signal.
3. A transparent reranker combines:

   - skill alignment: 50%;
   - core-skill coverage: 15%;
   - synthetic model fit: 15%;
   - experience proximity: 8%;
   - current-demand evidence grade: 5%; and
   - future-demand evidence grade: 7%.

4. A job is withheld unless skill alignment is at least 40%, core-skill coverage
   at least 25%, and the comparative score at least 55/100.

Demand grades are ordinal research mappings, not probabilities, vacancy shares,
or forecasts. The comparative score must not be described as confidence,
likelihood of success, or placement probability.

## Synthetic evaluation

The 28 July 2026 benchmark selected Extra Trees:

- mean synthetic accuracy: 0.7868;
- mean synthetic macro-F1: 0.7854.

These figures do not establish real-world accuracy. Full fold results and all
candidate scores are stored in `app/data/modelBenchmark.json` and regenerated by
`scripts/train_model.py`.

## Evidence controls

- Labor-market and AI statements are prewritten in a fixed source ledger.
- Current and future demand are separately labeled by evidence type.
- Certification and course links point to official provider pages.
- Practitioner accounts link to their original source, are labeled by source
  type, and include a causal caveat.
- Credentials do not replace supervised practice, portfolio evidence, licenses,
  or employer-specific requirements.

## Known limitations

- Synthetic profiles cannot represent real career transitions.
- Self-ratings can be inconsistent and affected by access to prior opportunities.
- Eleven job families omit many valid occupations.
- Years of experience do not prove seniority, leadership, or domain depth.
- Demand evidence is national, sector-level, or global; it is not a live vacancy
  feed and is not location-specific.
- Practitioner stories are vulnerable to issuer selection and survivorship bias.
- The browser deployment uses a transparent distance-weighted nearest-neighbor
  comparison over the same synthetic schema; the Streamlit and Colab versions
  train the benchmark-winning Extra Trees model.

## Production validation required

Before production use, obtain informed consent and governed real-world career
transition data; define time-bound outcomes; use licensed, dated vacancy data;
run external, temporal, calibration, and subgroup error tests; conduct research
with affected professionals; provide correction and appeal mechanisms; and
complete privacy, accessibility, legal, and employment-impact review.
