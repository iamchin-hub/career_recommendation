# Hakbang PH evidence ledger

Evidence snapshot: 28 July 2026

This ledger summarizes the fixed research layer used by the recommender. It
distinguishes direct evidence from inference. “Strong” and “Very strong” are
ordinal grades used for ranking; they are not vacancy shares or employment
probabilities.

## Cross-cutting findings

- The Philippine Statistics Authority reported that services represented 61.8%
  of employment in May 2026. This is sector context, not proof of demand for a
  particular job title: [PSA May 2026 Labor Force Survey](https://psa.gov.ph/statistics/labor-force-survey?vcode=sl76S8).
- DOLE's Jobs and Labor Market Forecast is the national source for key
  employment-generating sectors and in-demand, emerging, and hard-to-fill work:
  [DOLE forecast register](https://ble.dole.gov.ph/jobs-and-labor-market-forecast/).
- WEF's 2025 employer survey places big data, AI/ML, software, and security roles
  among the fastest-growing to 2030, while AI and big data, networks and
  cybersecurity, and technological literacy lead the rising skills:
  [jobs outlook](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/in-full/2-jobs-outlook/),
  [skills outlook](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/in-full/3-skills-outlook/).
- The ILO's 2025 task-level index supports describing GenAI mainly as a source of
  occupational transformation, with exposure varying by task and occupation—not
  as proof that every exposed job will disappear:
  [ILO refined index](https://www.ilo.org/publications/generative-ai-and-jobs-refined-global-index-occupational-exposure).

No credential guarantees a role, promotion, or salary. The recommended next step
is always to pair study with a role-relevant work sample and feedback from
practitioners or employers.
